Help

Questions we get a lot


How long does shipping take?

Orders start as pending, move to shipped, and end as delivered. You can follow the status on your My Orders page.

Can I return something?

Yes. If something isn't right, send us a message from the Contact page and we'll sort it out.

How do I pay?

Payments are not connected yet. Checkout records your order so we can see how the shop works.

Why can't I add more of an item to my cart?

The cart can't hold more than what we have in stock. Items with a "Sold out" label are gone for now.

Do I need an account?

You can browse without one. You need an account to check out, leave reviews and see your orders.

How do I change my email or password?

Log in and open Profile in the top bar.
